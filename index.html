About

Konstantin Wandel is a research fellow in the Social Data Science group at the University of Bielefeld and the Leibniz ScienceCampus SOEP-RegioHub (University of Bielefeld and DIW Berlin). Since September 2026 he is a doctoral researcher in sociology at the Bielefeld Graduate School in History and Sociology (BGHS).

His dissertation asks which modality carries the meaning in short-form video, and what follows for measurement once researchers code such material at scale. A second strand of his work builds the data infrastructure of SOEP-RegioHub: semantic search over survey and regional metadata, verified links into the portals that hold the data, and reproducible access paths for regionalized analysis. Further work covers content sharing in the German regional press, the spread of diets within households, and political language in large parliamentary corpora.

Dissertation

Short-Form Video as Data for the Social Sciences: Validated Multimodal Measurement of Online Communication (working title). Doctoral project in sociology at the BGHS since September 2026, supervised by Prof. Dr. Simon Kühne.

Four papers in argument order, from everyday communication to the political case. The instrument is validated on the more manifest everyday constructs first and taken to the latent political domain afterwards, which has the side benefit that the political round doubles as coder training.

Projects

Academic projects

Eating Together, Eating Alike

Household transmission and persistence of plant-based diets in Germany: how vegetarian and vegan diets spread between household members and how long they last, using the German Socio-Economic Panel (SOEP-Core, diet waves 2016–2023). Working paper with S. Kühne and K. Ostermann.

Content Sharing in the German Regional Press

A directed study of reprinting between regional newspapers, built on 62.7 million articles from 121 titles (2013–2024): which title carries another title’s text, how quickly, and along which lines. Same-day syndication dominates, flows follow size rather than wealth, ownership forms the backbone of the network, and local reporting stays siloed while national material travels. Only derived aggregates leave the licensed corpus.

Manuscripts in preparation: Where the News Is Made: The Vertical Division of Editorial Labour and the Distribution of Originality in the German Regional Press; A Public Sphere Blind to Its Cleavages: Ownership, Size, and the Geography of Content Sharing in the German Regional Press.

Semantic Drift in Parliamentary Speech

Embedding-based analysis of political language, topic structures, rurality, regional imaginaries, and semantic movement in large parliamentary corpora. With L. Gawenda.

Who speaks for the left-behind? Framing of spatial inequalities in the House of Commons (1988–2019)

Analysis of how spatial and regional inequalities are framed in British parliamentary discourse over three decades. With C. Zeng and L. Gawenda.

Tools

GeoDB

GeoDB, short for GeoDataBase, is a semantic search over the descriptions of German georeferenced data: about 12,500 indicators, tables and datasets from 41 sources, with filters over spatial level (municipality, district–NUTS3, NUTS2), theme, and year. Each hit links out to the portal that holds the data, wherever the portal allows it at the level of the single indicator or table. Descriptions are indexed, the data itself stays with its provider.

Open the tool

Archived release: 10.5281/zenodo.21134145

SOEP Variable Finder

Multilingual semantic search over the official SOEP-Core v41 variable metadata: 125,496 variables with bilingual labels, topic paths, and the question wording where it exists. A multilingual bi-encoder with a cross-encoder reranker surfaces canonical analysis-ready variables that keyword search tends to bury under chattier subsample items.

Open the tool

Archived release: 10.5281/zenodo.21134306

Parliamentary Speech Finder

Semantic search over 1,021,006 speeches in the German Bundestag, 1949–2025 (GermaParl), built on dense vector embeddings. It returns the original speeches ranked by similarity and generates no summaries. Supports research into semantic drift, topic modelling, and regional political imaginaries.

Open the tool

Archived release: 10.5281/zenodo.21194707

badata

R package with regional labour-market data from the German Federal Employment Agency: unemployed persons, employees and vacancies by district and occupational group, 2012–2025. With H. L. Nguyen and D. Tsolak; he extended the data through 2025 for version 0.2.0.
