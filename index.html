About

Konstantin Wandel is a research fellow in the Social Data Science group at the University of Bielefeld and the Leibniz ScienceCampus SOEP-RegioHub (University of Bielefeld and DIW Berlin). Since September 2026 he is a doctoral researcher in sociology at the Bielefeld Graduate School in History and Sociology (BGHS).

His dissertation asks which modality carries the meaning in short-form video, and what follows for measurement once researchers code such material at scale. A second strand of his work is research-data infrastructure for SOEP-RegioHub: search tools for survey and regional metadata that point to the portals holding the data. Further work covers content sharing in the German regional press, the spread of diets within households, and political language in parliamentary speech.

Dissertation

Short-Form Video as Data for the Social Sciences: Validated Multimodal Measurement of Online Communication (working title). Doctoral project in sociology at the BGHS since September 2026, supervised by Prof. Dr. Simon Kühne.

Four papers, from everyday communication to the political case: the measurement instrument is validated on everyday video first and then applied to political content.

Projects

Academic projects

Eating Together, Eating Alike

How vegetarian and vegan diets spread within households and how long they last, using the German Socio-Economic Panel. Working paper with S. Kühne and K. Ostermann.

Content Sharing in the German Regional Press

A study of reprinting between German regional newspapers: which titles share text with which, and how ownership, size and geography shape those flows. Only aggregated results leave the licensed corpus. Two manuscripts in preparation.

Semantic Drift in Parliamentary Speech

Embedding-based analysis of how political language and references to place change in parliamentary speech. With L. Gawenda.

Who speaks for the left-behind? Framing of spatial inequalities in the House of Commons (1988–2019)

Analysis of how spatial and regional inequalities are framed in British parliamentary discourse over three decades. With C. Zeng and L. Gawenda.

Metadata-to-RAG Infrastructure

Search and retrieval infrastructure for regionalized datasets, variable metadata, codebooks, and linked research documentation. The GeoDB and the SOEP Variable Finder run on it and index descriptions only.

Tools

GeoDB

GeoDB, short for GeoDataBase, is a semantic search over the descriptions of German regional data from statistical offices and other public sources, filterable by spatial level, theme and year. Each hit links to the portal that holds the data; only descriptions are indexed.

Open the tool

Archived release: 10.5281/zenodo.21134145

SOEP Variable Finder

Semantic search over the official SOEP-Core variable metadata, in German and English, with labels, topics and question wording.

Open the tool

Archived release: 10.5281/zenodo.21134306

Parliamentary Speech Finder

Semantic search over speeches in the German Bundestag since 1949 (GermaParl). It returns the original speeches ranked by similarity and generates no summaries.
